About us

Educating towards freedom

We are La Tribu, a community secondary school in Zapotal (near Mazunte). We learn through projects, collective work and our relationship with the environment. Here, education is built together by students, families and educators.

Workshops

What is learned with the hands

The garden

The cycles of the land, food sovereignty and respect for the Zapotal ecosystem. Nature as the first classroom.

Bamboo carpentry

Building with local materials: applied geometry, patience and the creation of useful objects for the community.

Music and arts

Rhythm, voice, drawing and color. Artistic expression as a way of knowing the world and knowing oneself.

Young entrepreneurs

Turning an idea into a real project: planning, creating and bringing the fruit of one's own work to the community.

How to enroll your child

1

Write to us

Send us a WhatsApp message or leave your details. Tell us your child's age and what you'd like to know.

2

We talk

We meet on a video call or in person, here in Zapotal. We answer all your questions, no rush.

3

We get to know each other

We meet with your family. Joining La Tribu means joining a community, and that is decided both ways.

4

Enrollment

We formalize the enrollment and accompany you in what comes next. We go over costs together in the FAQ.

We are forming the founding group. If you're looking for an active education, connected to nature and to making, for your children, this is the moment.
